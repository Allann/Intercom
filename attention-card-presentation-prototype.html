Why this is a real trade-off, not just a style choice

  • Native toast: durable (survives even if the app crashes before it's seen), shows in Action Center/notification history, respects Windows Focus Sessions and accessibility settings automatically — but the research doc is explicit that the 320×320 illustration is an asset source size, not a guaranteed rendered square; Windows owns the actual layout.
  • Custom popup: guarantees the comic-card look and exact size every time — but it's a second surface to build and maintain (focus stealing, multi-monitor placement, DPI scaling, its own dismiss/snooze state), doesn't appear in Action Center, and needs its own accessibility work Windows would otherwise give for free.
  • Recommendation to prototype further: native toast as the default durable path, reserving a custom popup only if the 320×320 comic-card visual is judged essential after seeing both side-by-side at real Windows DPI/text scales, per the resident-app research doc's own suggested next step.
